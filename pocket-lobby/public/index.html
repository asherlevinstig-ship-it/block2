        QUICK GUIDE

        Room controls.

        Your aim: wealth and power

        Become the richest and most powerful player in the game. Catch fish, chop trees and hunt monsters, then sell your supplies at the Trading Post in the Tower. Train your skills and improve your gear to earn more and defeat stronger monsters. Start by speaking to the Tower Warden in the Grand Lobby.

        Growing stronger

        Later skill levels take more XP. Train, upgrade equipment and return to harder encounters. The Depths offer better combat XP and mastery than woodland creatures. Your first victory after bonding earns an Awakening Stone, then every fifth victory earns another. The Vault Guardian and Essence Cache guarantee stones. Away work gathers supplies; dungeon materials require exploration.

        Explore

        Click a floor tile or use the arrow keys when the room is focused. Use your exit to reach the Grand Lobby, then use a door, the Outside steps, or the Tower directory to travel. Click a sofa or stool to sit. Use Wave or press Space while the room is focused to wave hello.

        Moonwood

        Take the courtyard steps into the forest. The field journal lists nearby trails and discoveries.

        Fishing

        Watch for the bite and click Reel, or press Space with the room focused, for 5 bonus Patience XP. Your character also reels automatically. Turn sound on at the pond for fishing cues.

        Inventory

        Choose Place to add furniture to the current room. Use Pick up to return a piece to storage.

        Decorate

        Use R to rotate. Press Escape to cancel a selection or return to Explore.

        Chat

        Type a message and press Enter. Chat reaches players in your current social room. Your home belongs to your saved character.

        01 / YOUR PLAN

        What should your adventurer do?

        Choose a goal, then fine-tune how it runs.

        AUTO GOAL

        Earn gold

        Choose suitable work, sell surplus and reassess after each small batch or fight. Hunting is limited to grounds you have already unlocked. No dungeon runs or gambling.

        Only bait packs may be purchased. Your bait reserve and timber target below still apply. Moonwell Shards, meals, gear and other materials are never sold by this goal. Work stops after eight hours or when no safe task is available.
        GAME GOLD ONLY

        Auto gambling

        Play with game gold at the Sunset Lounge. This can lose gold and is separate from Earn gold. Bets stay fixed; winnings do not reset your loss allowance.

        One round every 30 seconds. Stop before a full losing bet could exceed your loss limit or gold reserve. Blackjack hits below 17, then stands. Table odds and Luck XP rules are unchanged. Take control stops after completed rounds; closing the game keeps the same limits.

        Your activity

        Pick one activity or build a sequence below.

        Activity queue

        Gather, hunt, trade and rest in your chosen order. Fishing protects your bait reserve; chopping respects your timber target above.

        Compare hunting grounds
          Queue limits & supply detailsThe spending limit covers the whole queue, including every cycle. Bait is bought in packs of 10. Sell steps keep the quantity you specify and sell the rest. Shell Fragment sales never sell Moonwell Shards or other crafting items. Hunting needs an equipped weapon. Stoneback Beetles unlock after a manual victory in the Ruins. Both targets allow up to 20 fights per step, and skips remaining fights below your starting HP limit. The flee threshold below also applies when automatic spells are off. Flee attempts can fail. Each new idle period starts from step one. Up to 8 steps, 10 cycles and 8 hours.
