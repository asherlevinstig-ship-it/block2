    QUICK GUIDE

    Room controls.

    • Explore Click a floor tile or use the arrow keys when the room is focused. Use your exit to reach the Grand Lobby, then use a door, the Outside steps, or the Tower directory to travel. Click a sofa or stool to sit. Use Wave or press Space while the room is focused to wave hello.
    • Moonwood Take the courtyard steps into the forest. The field journal lists nearby trails and discoveries.
    • Fishing Watch for the bite and click Reel, or press Space with the room focused, for 5 bonus Patience XP. Your character also reels automatically. Turn sound on at the pond for fishing cues.
    • Inventory Choose Place to add furniture to the current room. Use Pick up to return a piece to storage.
    • Decorate Use R to rotate. Press Escape to cancel a selection or return to Explore.
    • Chat Type a message and press Enter. Chat reaches players in your current social room. Your home belongs to your saved character.

    While you’re away.

    Give your character a simple routine. After a minute away from the game, they follow it for up to eight hours. Return at any time to resume control.
