# Floor 1: Crowncleaver

The Crownkeeper now gives players a concrete repeat-clear reward in Fast World.

- Each eligible participant has a 10% personal chance of a Crowncleaver loot bag.
- Every eligible clear also banks one permanent Crown Fragment, independently of bag collection and backpack space.
- Five fragments and 30 gold forge Crowncleaver in the Town of Beginnings blacksmith's Weapons tab. The recipe appears after earning a fragment or owning the sword.
- Fragments live in saved character progress. Calling, loadout and attunement updates preserve the balance.
- Direct drops avoid owned or pending copies. Forging remains an explicit player choice.

Crowncleaver uses the sword combo with the same base 2/2/3 damage and slower 400/440/700 ms swings. Its third strike, Royal Break, can interrupt a windup even on a brute or champion (350 ms stagger; other enemies 650 ms). Existing stagger immunity and armoured enemy protection still apply. Active strikes are not cancelled.

The sword has gold crown fittings, a gold crescent attack effect and its own inventory icon. The existing collection, comparison and boss reward UI explain its benefit and source.

The Fast World smith now uses the shared position (79, 8, 136) for the NPC, client prompt and authoritative trade checks. Archived town interactions retain their original position.

Verification covers contribution eligibility, duplicate defeat prevention, unlucky-clear protection, full backpacks, personal drop ownership, duplicate drop suppression, forge rejection without spending, save rewrites and authoritative crescent damage/interrupts.
